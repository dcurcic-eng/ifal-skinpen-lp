WHAT IS SKINPEN

The First FDA-ClearedMicroneedling Pen

Fourteen fine needles create controlled micro-channels that prompt your skin to build its own collagen and elastin. No heat, no chemicals.

Because it works mechanically, SkinPen is safe for all skin tones, including Fitzpatrick IV to VI. It is a strong option if you are not a laser candidate.

What it treats

  • Acne scars: rolling, boxcar, ice pick
  • Fine lines and early aging
  • Neck wrinkles and crepey skin
  • Enlarged pores
  • Uneven texture
  • Hyperpigmentation and sun spots
  • Dull skin and loss of firmness

How SkinPen works,in three natural healing phases

Inflammation

Controlled micro-channels signal repair. Platelets and growth factors gather where your skin needs them.

Proliferation

Over the following weeks, new collagen, elastin and blood vessels begin to form.

Remodeling

Skin strengthens and reorganizes. Results keep improving for up to six months.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Your Visit, Start to Finish

Before your visit

5 to 7 days of prep

Pause retinoids, AHAs, BHAs and exfoliants 5 to 7 days ahead. Avoid sunburn for a week and arrive with clean skin.

During your visit

Up to an hour

Numbing cream for 20 to 30 minutes, then about 30 minutes of treatment. Most describe a mild vibration or a fine sandpaper feeling.

After your visit

2 to 3 days to routine

Mild redness and a sunburn-like feeling for 24 to 72 hours. Makeup after 24 hours. You go home with a personalized post-care kit.

Your treatment plan

3 to 6 sessions

Visible results in as few as three sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. Collagen keeps building for 3 to 6 months.
